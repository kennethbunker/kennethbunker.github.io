Chilean Political Landscape Dataset (CPLD)

Kenneth Bunker

Harvard Dataverse, version 4.0

DOIRequest PDFResearchGateAcademia.eduGoogle Scholar

Abstract

The Chilean Political Landscape Dataset (CPLD) compiles district-level indicators of electoral competition, party system structure, and institutional design across multiple decades of Chile's democratic experience.
